Responsive Website Development

Responsive web design has become need of the hour. According to stats, more than 90% of adults own a cell phone, whereas almost 50% of smartphone users admit that they could not live without their devices. In April 21, Google announced that mobile-friendliness was now a ranking symbol, meaning mobile-friendly, responsive websites appeared higher in the search results. We create “website for every screen” so that you can reach out to wider audiences, whether they are using desktop, tablet or mobile devices.
